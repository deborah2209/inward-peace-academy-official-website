Our story

Growing with purpose since 2011.

Inward Peace Academy was founded in 2011 with a commitment to helping every child grow through quality education, strong character and a deep sense of purpose.

Our school aims to create a warm and encouraging environment where children can develop their academic abilities, build good relationships and learn values that will guide them throughout life. We believe education should help children become thoughtful, responsible and compassionate members of their community.

We also nurture spiritual growth, good morals, respect, proper greetings and a strong awareness of the importance of preparing for the future. These lessons are woven into the daily life of the school so that every pupil can grow with confidence and integrity.

Our mission

Nurturing children in the fear and love of God, to become disciplined, responsible, and compassionate leaders, equipped to make a positive difference in their families, communities, and the world.

Our vision

Raising godly children with discipline for future leadership and to become confident, purpose-driven leaders who shine the light of Christ in every sphere of life.

Our approach

Children learn best when they feel known.

We value a balanced education that makes room for academic development, creativity, relationships and personal growth. A supportive partnership between school and family helps children feel encouraged as they take on new challenges.
