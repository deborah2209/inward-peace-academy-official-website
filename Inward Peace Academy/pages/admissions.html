The admission journey

Five steps to get started.

01

Enquiry

Tell us about your child and ask about classes, availability and what to expect.

02

Application

Get the application form from the school and gather the details and documents it requests.

03

Assessment

If an assessment is needed, the school will explain what it involves and how to prepare.

04

Admission offer

The school will contact you with its decision and explain the next steps if a place is offered.

05

Enrolment

Confirm your child's place with the school and complete any remaining enrolment requirements.

Before you apply

What to have ready.

To help your application proceed smoothly, it is useful to prepare the following information for the school office.

  • Completed admission enquiry or application form
  • Child's birth certificate or identification document
  • Previous school records, where applicable
  • Passport-sized photograph and relevant medical information

Frequently asked questions

What parents often want to know.

What age can my child apply?

Admissions are usually based on the child's age, class availability and the school's current entry requirements.

How do I apply?

Parents can make an enquiry through the school office and then follow the admission steps provided by the school.

Is an assessment required?

In some cases, an assessment may be part of the process. This will be confirmed during the admission enquiry.

How can I contact the school?

Use the contact page for the school's verified phone number, email and address.
